## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy outlines how the mobile application **Mini Game: Brick offline** ("App", "Game", "We") handles information during your use. This app is a free, casual retro block game designed primarily to work **offline**.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any data in compliance with the **Google Play Developer Policies**, **LGPD (Brazil)**, and **GDPR (European Union)**.

---

## 2. Information Collection and Local Storage

### 2.1. Information We DO NOT Collect
We design our apps with privacy by design:
- **No Account Required:** You do not need to register, create an account, or provide personal details (name, email, phone number) to play.
- **No Financial Data:** The app is free and does not process or request banking or credit card details.
- **No Sensitive Device Permissions:** The game does not request access to precise GPS location, camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, or private photo galleries.

### 2.2. Local Device Storage
The game only saves high scores, unlocked levels, and sound preferences directly on your device's internal storage (*SharedPreferences / Local Storage*).
- These records remain strictly on your physical phone and are never transmitted to our servers or third parties.
- Uninstalling the application or clearing app data in Android settings deletes this local data completely.

## 3. Third-Party Services and Advertising (Google AdMob)

To support the free availability and ongoing maintenance of the game, we integrate the **Google AdMob SDK** (Google Mobile Ads SDK) provided by Google LLC.

### 3.1. Data Processed by Google AdMob
Google AdMob may collect and process technical diagnostic and identifier information in order to serve, measure, and protect against fraudulent advertisements:
- **Google Advertising ID (AAID / GAID):** A resettable device advertising identifier.
- **Device & Diagnostic Data:** Device model, Android OS version, preferred language, network connectivity, and crash reports.
- **Ad Interaction Metrics:** Ad impressions, clicks, and engagement statistics.
- **Coarse Location:** Approximate location derived from IP address (city/country level) for regional ad relevance (no GPS is used).

## 6. User Rights & How to Opt-Out / Reset Advertising ID

You can manage or opt-out of personalized ads directly from Android settings:
1. Open **Settings** on your Android device.
2. Tap **Google** (or *Privacy > Ads*).
3. Tap **Ads**.
4. Select **Reset advertising ID** or **Delete advertising ID**.
